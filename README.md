# 🐾 Katziberts PetRadar

**Discover, research, and manage hunter pets in World of Warcraft Forever.**

Katziberts PetRadar is an in-game addon developed by **Katziberts**, a Horde guild in World of Warcraft Forever.

The addon combines a hunter pet encyclopedia with information about pet abilities, ability ranks, personal pet collections, and favorites.

## Overview

PetRadar is designed for hunters who want to explore the available tameable creatures in Azeroth and make informed decisions about their pets.

Rather than functioning as a simple creature tracker, PetRadar focuses on providing a searchable knowledge base.

Players can research specific pets, discover which creatures offer particular abilities, and keep track of their own pets.

## Planned Features

### Pet Encyclopedia

A searchable database of hunter pets available in World of Warcraft Forever.

Planned information includes:

- Pet names and creature information
- Locations where pets can be found
- Pet abilities
- Available ability ranks
- Pet level information

### Pet Search

Find suitable pets based on specific requirements.

Search options are planned for:

- Specific pets
- Desired pet abilities
- Ability ranks
- Pet levels

This allows hunters to research which creatures offer the abilities and ranks they are looking for.

### Pet Radar

A radar-style feature for locating specific hunter pets.

The radar is intended to complement the encyclopedia by helping players search for creatures of interest.

Its implementation will depend on the capabilities of the World of Warcraft Forever addon interface.

### Personal Stable Overview

Keep an overview of owned hunter pets and their abilities.

Planned information includes:

- Owned pets
- Known pet abilities
- Ability ranks
- Current pet information

The goal is to provide a convenient overview of the player's pets without having to inspect them individually.

### Favorites

Save interesting pets from the encyclopedia for later.

Hunters can mark pets they want to research, find, or tame in the future.

Favorites provide a personal collection of potential pets without requiring them to be owned.

## World of Warcraft Integration

PetRadar is designed specifically for the World of Warcraft Forever client.

The addon runs directly inside World of Warcraft and uses the interfaces available to in-game addons.

Some features, particularly radar functionality and automatic access to pet information, depend on the capabilities and limitations of the supported client.

## Distribution

PetRadar will be available through two independent download options.

### Katziberts Addon Manager

Players can discover, download, install, and update PetRadar through the Katziberts Addon Manager for Windows.

### Katziberts Website

PetRadar will also be available as an individual addon download through the official Katziberts website.

Using the Addon Manager is optional.

Players who install the addon manually are responsible for checking for updates and installing new versions themselves.
